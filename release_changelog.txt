## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.22

**Gravatar on the contact cards (optional).**

- **Settings → General → Contact cards → Gravatar**: a contact without a photo shows the Gravatar of its first email address on its card. Only shown, never saved in the note or on the card; a contact's own photo always wins.
- **Without a Gravatar** chooses what the card shows when there is none: the initials (default), or an image Gravatar makes (silhouette, identicon, monster, wavatar, retro, robot, empty).
- Off by default: Gravatar receives a hash of the email address of every contact you open.
