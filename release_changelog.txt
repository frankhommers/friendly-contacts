## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.14

**Sync all contacts: one list of everything that differs.** Run the command **Sync all contacts**, or click the new sync button in the contacts sidebar. A tab opens that checks every contact note against its card.

- Left: each contact that differs, with what is going on: **From <address book>**, **From Obsidian**, **Conflict**, **New card** or **Check warning**. Above it: how many are up to date.
- Right: the same review as **Sync this contact**, for the selected contact. Pick per difference, then **Apply**, or go on with **Previous** and **Next**.
- **Apply all that are ready** applies every contact with the choices shown for it. It skips open conflicts, new cards (send those one by one) and contacts whose review carries a warning.
- Before anything is written, the contact is checked again. If the note or the card changed since the scan, nothing is written and the contact shows again with "Changed since the scan; check it again."
- Contacts that cannot be synced are listed at the bottom with the reason. **Scan again** checks everything anew.

**Turn Sync off for a note:** put `sync: false` in its properties. Sync all leaves it out, and **Sync this contact** says why it does nothing.
