## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.13

**Photos on vCard 4.0 cards on Radicale are no longer lost.** Radicale damages a photo on a vCard 4.0 card: it comes back without the image. Until now the next Sync put that damaged value into your note, and the photo was gone on both sides.

- Sync recognises a damaged photo and never puts it into your note. An edit elsewhere on the card leaves your note's photo alone, and a photo you add, change or remove in the note is written once, without a Sync loop.
- A phone cannot show such a photo; when it sends the card back without it, that is not taken for a removal.
- A note an earlier version already damaged is put right at its next Sync: the review says so, and Apply removes the damaged value or puts back the photo of the last Sync.
- Phones still get no photo from a 4.0 card on Radicale: set **New contacts** to **vCard 3.0** there. vCard 3.0 cards were never affected.

Update the plugin on every desktop that shares the vault: an older version keeps writing the damaged photo into your notes. Back up your vault before installing.
