## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.19

**Default contact fields moved to the Properties tab.**

- Every property has an **In new contacts** switch in its details: on, and a new contact gets the property, empty and ready to fill in.
- At the bottom of the Properties tab, **New contacts** lists those properties; click one to open its details. Card fields without a property (such as `X-SKYPE`) can still be added and removed there, and **Reset to defaults** brings back the default fields.
- Renaming a property now carries over to the fields of new contacts, and a new contact gets its first and last name under the names you gave them (it always wrote `firstname` and `lastname`).
