## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.17

**Fix: the property table (Settings → Property names) no longer makes the settings scroll sideways.**

- Fixed texts break at sensible places: `RELATED;TYPE=…` after the semicolon, and keys such as `X-ABRELATEDNAMES` stay whole. The Part column is narrow.
- In a narrow window the Name column gets narrower and long texts may break anywhere, instead of a scrollbar.
