## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.20

**Properties of your own, and dates with a label in the property list.**

- **Add a property** (under the property list) takes any card field no property reads yet, such as `X-BANAAN` or `TEL[FAX]`, and a name for your notes. It shows in the list with all its settings (name, Read from, Write as, In new contacts, Sync) and is synced from then on; values already on the cards come in at the next Sync.
- Adding a card field under **New contacts** does the same, so it is synced too (in 3.0 such a field stayed in Obsidian only). A field an earlier version stored without a property gets a **Make property** button.
- **Dates with a label** (such as Verkering → `dating`) are in the list under Dates, with their details. **Remove** for them and for your own properties is in the details. The Dates with a label block keeps adding a label and finding labels on cards.
