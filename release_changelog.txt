## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.2 (second test release)

This test release adds a folder per contact, relationships in the sidebar and a people graph. It is still an alpha: please try it on a copy of your vault first.

### Before you install

- **Back up your vault**, at least `.obsidian/plugins/friendly-contacts/data.json`.
- Coming from VCF Contacts: settings migrate **one way** to the list of address books.
- **Sync works on desktop only.** The sidebar, relationships and the people graph also work on mobile.

### New in this release

- **A folder per contact (optional).** Each person can get their own folder with the contact note as folder note, so poems, speeches and photos live next to them. Choose the folder note name (`{{name}}` or a fixed name such as `index`) and whether it sits inside or next to the folder, or copy the settings from the Folder Notes plugin. Commands **Put each contact in its own folder** and **Take contacts out of their folders** never touch other files; folders are only removed when empty. **Move contacts to folder…** moves whole folders, and renaming through Sync renames folder and note together.
- **Relationships in the sidebar.** Each contact shows its relationships by kind. **+ Relationship** picks a person and a kind, **×** removes one, and mirroring updates the other person. People who are only on the server get an **Import** button (or **Link** when they already have a note). Also as the command **Add relationship…**.
- **People graph.** A view of people and how they relate, centred on you ("This is me" in the settings) or on a contact. Lines are coloured by kind (partner, family, friends, work, other) and labelled from the viewing person's side. Choose the depth, filter kinds, drag and zoom; tap the person in the middle (or **Open note**) to open their note. People without a note show dashed and can be imported.

### Also in this release (from 3.0.0-alpha.1)

- Friendly property names, lossless CardDAV sync per contact with a review screen (nothing is written before Apply), several address books with `source:` per contact, **Import contact from server…**, relationships as `[[links]]` with mirroring and **Check relationships**, birthdays without a year (`--MM-DD`).

### Known limits

- Not yet tried inside a running Obsidian by the author on every platform; especially the people graph on mobile needs your feedback.
