## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.12

**Removals on your phone stick, also with several desktops.** After the update to alpha.11 a desktop that had not yet synced a contact could put back a part (a department, a prefix, a date with a label) that you had removed on your phone, because another desktop had already filled it into your note. Sync now sees that your note already held the card's old value and takes the removal from the phone.

Everything from 3.0.0-alpha.11 still applies: every part of a card field (name prefix and suffix, department, gender and gender identity, PO box and extra address line) is a property in your notes, and the property names table has Part and vCard 4.0 / 3.0 write columns.

Update the plugin on every desktop before you sync from it. Back up your vault before installing.
