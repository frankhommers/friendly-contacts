## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.9

**Dates with a label.** A date your phone keeps under a label of its own, such as "Verkering", now shows in the note under an English property you choose, for example `dating: 2012-05-01`. A date you fill in in Obsidian goes to the card with that label, so the phone shows it under "Verkering".

- Settings → Property names → **Dates with a label**: add a label with its property, rename it, or remove it. **Find labels on cards** lists the labels already on your contacts' cards (as at their last Sync).
- Adding a label fills the dates into your notes from the last Sync, after you confirm. Sync never removes such a date from a card because the note did not have it yet, also on your other devices.
- Removing a label removes the property from your notes after you confirm. The dates stay on the cards.
- Works on vCard 3.0 and 4.0 cards. Dates without a label (Android "Other" without text) are not shown.

Back up your vault before installing.
