## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.4

- The address book settings are clearer: each address book is a framed block with its name, sync status and Enable/Disable at the top; "Default for new contacts" is a switch with an explanation; removing an address book is its own row (not possible for the default one, with the reason shown).
- From alpha.3: the settings no longer show "[object DocumentFragment]".

Everything else is as in 3.0.0-alpha.2. Back up your vault before installing.
