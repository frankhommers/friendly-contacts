## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.1 (first test release)

This is the first test release of Friendly Contacts, a fork of VCF Contacts. It is an alpha: please try it and tell me what breaks.

### Before you install

- **Back up your vault**, at least `.obsidian/plugins/friendly-contacts/data.json`.
- Settings migrate **one way**: your old single CardDAV address book becomes the first entry of a new list of address books. Going back to an older version is not supported.
- **Sync works on desktop only.** On mobile you can use the contact notes, but not sync.
- This is an alpha. Expect rough edges.

### What is new compared to VCF Contacts

- **Friendly property names.** Contact notes use readable English properties (`mobile`, `work-email`, `birthday`, `children`) instead of `TEL[CELL]` or `ADR[HOME].STREET`. A mapping table in the settings shows which property belongs to which vCard field, and you can rename them.
- **Lossless CardDAV sync, per contact.** Only what you changed is written to the server; the rest of the card (Apple labels, types, unknown fields) stays exactly as it was. Sync opens a **review screen** that shows the address book value and the note value side by side. Nothing is written before you press Apply, and where both sides changed you decide the result yourself.
- **Several address books.** Add as many CardDAV address books as you like. Each contact note names its book in `source:`, and you can move a contact to another book by changing `source:` and syncing. You pick a default book for new contacts.
- **Import contact from server…** Pick a single card that has no note yet, from any of your address books.
- **Relationships as `[[links]]`.** `spouse`, `children`, `parents` and the like are links between notes, and are mirrored between the two notes. The **Check relationships** command lists relationships that are only noted on one side and lets you add the missing side or remove the link.
- **Birthdays without a year.** A birthday with no year is shown as `--MM-DD` and written back to the card in the form the card used (including the Apple form).
- **Move contacts to folder…** Moves all your contact notes to a folder you choose.

### Not in this release

Folder per contact, relationships in the sidebar and the people graph are planned for later.
