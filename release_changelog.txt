## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.18

**New look for the settings: tabs, and a property list instead of the big table.**

- The plugin settings have three tabs: **General**, **Sync** (address books, links to Obsidian notes on cards) and **Properties**. The settings open on the tab you used last.
- **Properties** lists your property names in groups (Name, Phone, email and web, Work, Address, Dates, Relationships, Other), each with a Sync switch. Click one to see and change its details beside the list: name in the note, Read from, Part, Write as (vCard 4.0 and 3.0) and Sync, each with a short explanation.
- In a narrow window the details open right under the property you clicked.
- Nothing changes in what is stored: the same property names, the same Sync setting.
