## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.11

**Every part of a card field is visible.** Name prefix and suffix, department, gender and gender identity, PO box and extra address line (home and work) are now properties in your notes. Values already on your cards arrive in your notes at the next Sync; nothing is removed from a card.

- The property names table in the settings has a **Part** column (which part of a field made of parts, counting from 1: `ORG` part 2 is the department) and two write columns: **Write as (vCard 4.0)** and **Write as (vCard 3.0)**. Leave the 3.0 column empty to use the 4.0 field. Gender is written as `GENDER` on 4.0 cards and `X-GENDER` on 3.0 cards.
- The new rows are added once to your existing table, next to the rows they belong to.
- `gender` is one letter (`M`, `F`, `O`, `N`, `U`); `gender-identity` is free text. Phones do not show the gender.
- Radicale stores `GENDER:O;non-binary` with the `;` escaped; the plugin reads both the same way.
- Changing **Read from** or **Part** only changes how cards are read from now on; it does not refill your notes.
- Update the plugin on every desktop before you sync from it: an older version would offer to remove the new parts from your cards.

Back up your vault before installing.
