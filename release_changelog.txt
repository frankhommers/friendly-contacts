## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.8

**Relationship links show the name, not a path.** With Obsidian's link format set to "Relative path to file" or "Absolute path in vault", a relationship showed as `../Ger Verhallen/Ger Verhallen`. Such links now get the contact's name as label: `[[../Ger Verhallen/Ger Verhallen|Ger Verhallen]]`, so the property shows "Ger Verhallen".

- New links get this form everywhere: Sync, "+ Relationship", the other side of a relationship, and putting contacts in their own folders.
- **Tidy relationship links** (command palette) does it once for the links already in your notes. A label you wrote yourself is kept, and only relationship properties of contact notes change.

Back up your vault before installing.
