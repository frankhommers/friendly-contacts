## Friendly Contacts 3.0.0-alpha.5

- "Insights" is now **Import** (inbox icon in the sidebar header): contacts on your address books that have no note yet, grouped per address book with a search field (also finds "René" when you type "rene"). Import one, all of one address book, or everything at once (with a confirmation). The "Group insights" switch is gone.
- The background checks in the settings have plain names.
- From alpha.4: clearer address book settings. From alpha.3: no more "[object DocumentFragment]" in the settings.

Known issue: relationships are not yet written the way DAVx5 (Android) and Apple store them; relationships set on the phone are not read yet. Avoid editing relationships on contacts you also edit on your phone until this is fixed.

Back up your vault before installing.
